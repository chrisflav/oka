# oka

A formalization in Lean 4 / Mathlib of **Oka's coherence theorem** and of **Serre's GAGA**, with
their consequences for the étale fundamental group, and some algebraic geometry over complete
discrete valuation rings and uniformisation of punctured elliptic curves used by other
lana-agents projects.

Authors: Yuichiro Hoshi, Junnosuke Koizumi, Christian Merten.

License: Apache 2.0 (see `LICENSE`).

## Main results

* **Complex analytic spaces** (`ComplexAnalytic.AnalyticSpace`, `Oka/AnalyticSpace/Basic.lean`):
  locally ringed spaces locally isomorphic to closed analytic subspaces of open subsets of `ℂⁿ`.
* **Oka's coherence theorem** (`ComplexAnalytic.AnalyticSpace.isCoherentStructureSheaf`,
  `Oka/AnalyticSpace/Coherent.lean`): the structure sheaf of any complex analytic space is
  coherent.
* **Analytification** (`ComplexAnalytic.analytification`, `Oka/Analytification/Scheme.lean`):
  the functor `X ↦ X^an` from schemes locally of finite type over `ℂ` to complex analytic spaces.
* **Serre's GAGA for proper schemes** (`ComplexAnalytic.gagaFull_proper`,
  `Oka/Analytification/GAGA/Proper/Equivalence.lean`): for a proper scheme `X` over `ℂ`,
  `Hᵠ(X, F) ≅ Hᵠ(X^an, F^an)` for coherent `F`, and analytification is an equivalence between
  coherent sheaves on `X` and coherent analytic sheaves on `X^an`
  (`ComplexAnalytic.gagaEquivalenceOfIsProperℂ`). The proof reduces to the projective case via
  Chow's lemma and uses Rückert's Nullstellensatz.
* **The Riemann existence theorem** (`ComplexAnalytic.riemannExistenceTheoremEquiv`,
  `Oka/Analytification/RET/RiemannExistence.lean`): for every scheme `X` locally of finite type
  over `ℂ`, analytification is an equivalence between finite étale covers of `X` and finite étale
  covers of `X^an` with separated structure map. The proof extends covers across boundary divisors
  by a formalisation of the Grauert–Remmert extension theorem and algebraises them with GAGA.
* **The comparison theorem `π₁ᵉᵗ ≅ π₁^top(X^an)^`**
  (`ComplexAnalytic.etaleFundamentalGroupEquivCompletion`,
  `Oka/Analytification/Pi1/General.lean`): for every connected scheme `X` locally of finite type
  over `ℂ` (possibly singular, non-reduced or non-separated) and `x : X^an`, the étale fundamental
  group of `X` at the geometric point at `x` (`Aut (AlgebraicGeometry.FiniteEtale.fiber ξₓ)`,
  from pi1) is isomorphic as a topological group to the profinite completion of the topological
  fundamental group `π₁(X^an, x)`. The proof combines the Riemann existence theorem, a
  covering-space construction for subgroups of `π₁` (`Oka/Topology/Covering/PathCover.lean`), the
  criterion `CategoryTheory.PreGaloisCategory.isFundamentalGroup_completion`, and local
  contractibility of `X^an` (`ComplexAnalytic.locallyContractibleSpace_analytification`), proved
  by Łojasiewicz's gradient-flow retraction onto real algebraic sets
  (`Oka/Analysis/Lojasiewicz/`) from the Łojasiewicz gradient inequality
  (`MvPolynomial.exists_lojasiewicz_gradient`, `Oka/Geometry/RealAlgebraic/`, via Tarski–Seidenberg
  and the monotonicity theorem for semialgebraic functions). The analytic analogue is
  `ComplexAnalytic.AnalyticSpace.SeparatedFiniteEtaleOver.fundamentalGroupContinuousMulEquiv`.
* **Serre's finiteness theorem on projective space**
  (`AlgebraicGeometry.ProjectiveSpace.cechFinite_of_isCoherent`,
  `Oka/AlgebraicGeometry/ProjectiveSpace/SerreFiniteness.lean`): for `R` noetherian and `F` coherent
  on `ℙⁿ_R`, the Čech cohomology of `F` for the standard cover is a finitely generated `R`-module in
  every degree.
* **Zariski's connectedness theorem** (`AlgebraicGeometry.ProjectiveSpace.isPreconnected_closedFibre`,
  `Oka/AlgebraicGeometry/ProjectiveSpace/ZariskiConnected.lean`): an integral closed subscheme of
  `ℙⁿ_R`, for `R` a complete discrete valuation ring, whose generic point lies over the generic
  point of `Spec R` has a connected closed fibre. The proof uses Serre finiteness and the
  degree-zero part of the theorem on formal functions (`Plans/ZariskiConnected.md`).
* **Uniformisation of once-punctured elliptic curves** (`Oka/Uniformization/`,
  `Plans/Uniformization.md`, piece U1):
  `Uniformization.uniformization_oncePunctured` gives, for every elliptic curve `E` over `ℂ`, a
  holomorphic universal covering `ℍ → E(ℂ) ∖ {O}` whose deck group is generated by
  `A, B ∈ SL(2, ℝ)` with `tr(ABA⁻¹B⁻¹) = −2`. It is built with a Koebe iteration and the modular
  `j`-function, and does not use the general uniformisation theorem. It is unique up to conjugation
  in `SL(2, ℝ)` (`Uniformization.IsUniformization.unique`).
* **Commensurators and finite étale covers of `E ∖ O`** (`Plans/U2.md`, piece U2): cusp geometry
  and growth estimates; `Γ`-invariant holomorphic functions of polynomial growth are regular
  functions (`Uniformization.FEt.exists_coord_of_invariant`); automorphisms of finite étale covers
  lift to Möbius transformations (`Uniformization.FEt.exists_mobius`); and R3: if the deck group
  `Γ̃` has finite index in its commensurator, then the commensurator is `Γ̃` unless `g₃ = 0` or
  `g₂ = 0` (`Uniformization.Peripheral.commensurator_eq_pmDeck_or`). These are the complex-analytic
  inputs of `CanLift27` over `ℂ`, which is assembled in `orbicurve-cores`.

All of these are proved without `sorry`.

## Dependencies

Lean 4 `leanprover/lean4:v4.32.0`, Mathlib `v4.32.0`, and (`lakefile.toml`):

* [`pi1`](https://github.com/lana-agents/pi1) at `4996dc3`: the étale fundamental group of a scheme
  (`AlgebraicGeometry.FiniteEtale.galoisCategory`, `AlgebraicGeometry.FiniteEtale.fiber`), the
  algebraic side of the comparison theorem in `Oka/Analytification/Pi1/`.
* [`heights`](https://github.com/lana-agents/heights) at `3539e2a`: the modular `j`-function with
  its fibres and the lattice parametrisation of Weierstrass curves over `ℂ`, used in
  `Oka/Uniformization/`.
