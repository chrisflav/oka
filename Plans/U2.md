## 1. The dictionary (over `ℂ`)

Fix `π : ℍ → V = E(ℂ) ∖ O` (affine points) from U1, `Γ = ⟨A, B⟩ ⊆ SL(2, ℝ)`, `O(X) = ℂ[x, y]/(W)`,
`xπ = x ∘ π`, `yπ = y ∘ π` holomorphic on `ℍ`.

U2.1 **Covering and uniqueness** (oka). `π` is a covering map onto `V` (fibres = orbits,
immersion, `V` a complex curve). Any two uniformisations `(A, B, π)`, `(A', B', π')` of `W` differ
by `g ∈ SL(2, ℝ)`: `π' = π ∘ g`, `±⟨A', B'⟩ = g⁻¹ (±⟨A, B⟩) g`. (Lift `π'` through `π`; the lift is
biholomorphic, hence Möbius, `Uniformization.exists_SL2R_eq_of_holomorphic`.)

U2.2 **Cusp geometry** (oka). The lift `Σ` of `exp` near the puncture gives an injective
holomorphic `q = G(w)`, `G(0) = 0`, `G′(0) ≠ 0`, with `M⁻¹ Σ(log w) = t log G(w) / 2πi` for a
normalising `M ∈ SL(2, ℝ)`; the horoball `M(Im > h)` is precisely invariant under `⟨C⟩`; `ℍ` is
`Γ · (compact ∪ horoball)`. Consequences: two-sided comparison
`c₁ e^{D(τ)} − c₂ ≤ log (1 + |xπ(τ)|) ≤ c₃ e^{D(τ)} + c₄`, `D(τ) = d_ℍ(Γ τ, τ₀)`; and
`D(δ τ) ≤ D(τ) + c_δ` for `δ ∈ Comm Γ` (finite index, no cusp classification needed).

U2.3 **Algebraic functions** (oka). A `Γ`-invariant holomorphic `g` with
`log |g| ≤ c e^{D} + c'` is a polynomial in `xπ, yπ` (descend to an elliptic function with a pole
of bounded order at `0`; `℘`, `℘′` basis). Hence for `δ ∈ Comm Γ`, `xπ ∘ δ`, `yπ ∘ δ` are integral
over `O(X)` (symmetric functions over `Γ / (Γ ∩ δ⁻¹Γδ)`).

U2.4 **Riemann existence dictionary** (oka). `X = Spec O(X)` as `SchemeLFTℂ`; `X^an ≃ₜ V`;
`FEt(X) ≌ SepFEt(X^an)` (oka RET) `≌` finite coverings of `V` `≌` finite `Γ`-sets. For
`Δ ≤ Γ` of finite index: `O(Z_Δ) ≅ {h ∈ Hol(ℍ)^Δ integral over O(X)}` (the inclusion is RET; the
equality uses that a connected scheme has connected analytification: an integral element gives a
finite cover of `Z_Δ` with a global analytic section, hence of degree `1`).

U2.5 **Orbicurves** (orbicurve-cores). Objects of `\overline{Loc}_ℂ(X)` ↔ subgroups `Δ ⊆ PSL(2, ℝ)`
commensurable with `Γ` (coordinate ring `Hol(ℍ)^Δ ∩ integral`, multiplicities = stabiliser
orders); `Hom Y Y′ ↔ {g : g Δ_Y g⁻¹ ⊆ Δ_{Y′}} / Δ_{Y′}`. Correspondences ↔ `Comm Γ`
(`δ ↦ (Z_{Γ ∩ δ⁻¹Γδ}, pr₁, δ)`, algebraic by U2.3).

U2.6 **Cores** (orbicurve-cores). `X` has a `ℂ`-core ⇔ `[Comm Γ : Γ] < ∞`; the core is
`Comm Γ \ ℍ`. With R3: `hemi E` is the core when `Γ` is not arithmetic.

U2.7 **Base change** (orbicurve-cores / pi1). Cores are geometric and invariant under extension of
algebraically closed fields of characteristic `0`; `k → k̄ → ℂ` (for `k` of cardinality at most
the continuum directly; in general through a finitely generated field of definition of `E`).
`GenuineCoreBaseChange` follows from the classification (`CanLift27` + `CanLift27Arith`) and the
compatibility of `Genuine.realize` with base change.

## 2. Refined route to `CanLift27` over `ℂ` (no Fuchsian area theory)

Write `x = ℘∘ψ` (the coordinate of `hemi E`), `Γ̃ = pmDeck`.

* **Theorem G.** Let `W → X` be finite étale (`W` a scheme), `C` an affine orbicurve and
  `W → C` finite étale. If `Comm Γ = Γ̃`, then `x ∈ A_C` (inside `A_W`). Proof: Galois closure
  `W'` of `K_W / K_C` (orbifold-étale over `C`, hence étale over `W` and `X`: inertia groups are
  cyclic in characteristic `0` and the core of `H` in `I` is trivial); embed `A_{W'} ↪ Hol(ℍ)`
  over `A_X` (P2); every `σ ∈ Gal(K_{W'}/K_C)` is `f ↦ f ∘ δ_σ` with `δ_σ ∈ N(Γ_{W'}) ⊆ Comm Γ = Γ̃`
  (P3); `x ∘ δ = x`, so `x ∈ K_C` and `x ∈ A_C`.
* **Existence** of `Y → hemi E` for `Y ∈ LocBar X` (`Z → X`, `Z → Y`): Theorem G with `W = Z`,
  `C = Y`; the multiplicity condition by multiplicativity of ramification.
* **Uniqueness**: a second map `u` gives, by Theorem G, `x = p(u)`, and `p : A¹ → A¹` is étale for
  the hemi multiplicities (`2` at the roots `e_i`). Riemann–Hurwitz for polynomials
  (`Σ (e_w - 1) = deg p - 1`) forces `deg p = 1`; an affine map permuting `{e_i}` is the identity
  unless `j ∈ {0, 1728}`.
* **R3** (`Comm Γ = Γ̃` when `[Comm Γ : Γ] < ∞`): for `N = Comm Γ`, the `N`-invariant polynomials
  in `x` form `ℂ[t]` (symmetric functions over `N / Γ̃` are polynomials in `x` by
  `eq_poly_of_bound`; Lüroth); `hemi → Spec ℂ[t]` is étale for suitable multiplicities, and the
  polynomial Riemann–Hurwitz count gives `Σ (1 - 1/m) = 1 + 1/(2d)`, i.e. `d = 1`, or `d = 2`
  with signature `(2, 4, ∞)` (then `j = 1728`), or `d = 3` with `(2, 3, ∞)` (then `j = 0`).
* **P1** `A_X ↪ Hol(ℍ)` and `X(ℂ) = V`; **P2** finite étale `A_X → B` gives a covering
  `B(ℂ) → X(ℂ)` with holomorphic local sections, hence `B ↪ Hol(ℍ)` (lift of `ψ`); **P3**
  automorphisms of `B` over `A_C` lift to `ℍ` (Möbius by `HalfPlaneAut`).
* `AdmitsCore Γ` for non-exceptional `j`: orbicurve-cores `canLift27_group_unconditional` plus S1.
* General `k`: Lefschetz (finitely generated field of definition, embedding into `ℂ`) and descent
  of the unique map.
